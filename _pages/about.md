I am a Staff Research Scientist at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/), where I work on 3D coding and de-rendering, spatial intelligence, and multimodal understanding for Gemini. Our work was featured on the [official blog post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-argon/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=&utm_content=) and [announcement](https://lnkd.in/p/gpeXXwBu) of Gemini 4 Argon and [Carbon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/). I received my D.Phil. (Ph.D.) in Engineering Science from the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), supervised by Prof. [Philip Torr](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~phst/) and Prof. [Victor Prisacariu](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~victor/).
