Staff Research Scientist · Google DeepMind

I am a Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, where I work on 3D coding and de-rendering, spatial intelligence, and multimodal understanding for Gemini. Our work was featured on the official blog post and announcement of Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon. I received my D.Phil. (Ph.D.) in Engineering Science from the University of Oxford, supervised by Prof. Philip Torr and Prof. Victor Prisacariu.
